Privacy policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

Your study information stays on your device unless you choose a backup, export or connected service. TestFlight separately collects beta diagnostics. Local study does not require an FRCS Companion account.

Who operates the app

FRCS Companion is operated by Deepinder Singh Dhillon, an independent individual developer in England, United Kingdom, not a registered limited company. I am responsible for the processing I control described below, including my handling of support and beta feedback. Contact frcscompanion.support@gmail.com for support and privacy requests.

Your device and study information

The app stores information you enter or import: exam settings, courses, tasks, notes, chapters, viva practice records and scores, imported conversations, PDFs and their catalogue, preferences, and an optional display name and profile photo. Local recovery copies retain earlier records. A shared on-device App Group supplies study information to the six widgets.

New installations hide personal widget details until you enable them. Widgets may be visible to other people. The system photo picker accesses the image you select; reminders use local notifications with your permission. Device-level iCloud or computer backups may separately include app data according to your settings. Do not enter identifiable patient information, and only import material you are entitled to use.

Optional iCloud and Google Drive backup

You choose and review a destination before enabling backup. Once enabled, changed records may be uploaded while the app is active and connected without asking for each transfer. Backups include the study snapshot, profile/settings and catalogued PDFs, including archived PDFs. These are versioned recovery copies, not continuous two-way synchronisation.

  • Apple iCloud / CloudKit: uses your device's iCloud account and that user's private CloudKit database. Apple processes stored records, document payloads and account information needed for storage. This does not create an FRCS account or use an in-app Sign in with Apple login.
  • Google Drive: uses Google Sign-In and your approval of app-specific Drive storage. The app accesses its hidden application-data folder, not your general Drive library. The SDK handles authorisation and tokens. The app uses your Google user identifier to check the destination and stores a derived account identifier with backup confirmation metadata. Google states that its sign-in SDK may collect user identifiers and IP addresses, including for fraud prevention.

Backup receipts include version identifiers, timestamps and integrity fingerprints. Credentials are not included in study archives. Provider storage limits, availability and policies apply: Apple privacy, Google privacy and Google Sign-In data disclosure.

Exports and the optional private service

Complete manual archives contain records and PDF files; JSON recovery snapshots do not include PDF binary files. Portable archives are not encrypted by this app. Protect them and choose a private destination: a recipient may be able to read the contents.

This beta update disables AI tutoring. It does not send AI tutoring requests to OpenAI or another AI provider, and it does not connect to your ChatGPT account or use your ChatGPT subscription. Local study, viva practice and previously saved material remain available. Optional non-AI record synchronisation still requires you to configure your own HTTPS service address and access token. This private-service connection does not synchronise automatically. Separate actions upload selected records or a study snapshot (including document metadata, not PDF binaries), fetch records for review, or request deletion of a server record. These actions send data to your configured service; check its operator’s privacy and retention terms before connecting. Disconnecting removes local connection credentials, not server records or logs. No hosted private service is supplied with the app.

Earlier releases, including version 1.0 (27), included an optional tutor that required a separately configured service. In those releases, enabling the tutor’s consent control and tapping Send sent messages and recent conversation context to that service, which could forward them to OpenAI. Updating the app disables that tutor function but does not delete information previously sent to a service or its providers. Contact the relevant service operator about its retention and deletion procedures.

TestFlight, feedback and support

TestFlight automatically collects beta crash and usage information and makes information available to the developer. Depending on testing and feedback, this can include device/OS and app versions, crash logs, session information, name/email, comments and screenshots. This is separate from the app's Share feedback button. See TestFlight & Privacy.

Share feedback opens the system share sheet with your text and app version/build; it does not automatically attach study records. Email support receives your address, message and anything you attach. I use these to respond, resolve problems and improve reliability; Gmail processes correspondence. Exclude patient details, credentials and private documents.

Retention and deletion

  • Local information: remains while you use the app. Removing current records or archiving PDFs does not erase earlier recovery copies or every PDF. There is no in-app purge-all function. Deleting the app ordinarily removes its local sandbox, but shared-container data, device backups, exports and Keychain authorisations can have different lifecycles.
  • Cloud versions: are deliberately retained; there is no in-app cloud-backup deletion function or automatic retention limit. Stop automatic backup prevents future scheduled transfers, but an in-flight transfer may already have completed. Forget Drive authorisation signs out locally; it does not delete backups or necessarily revoke the Google grant.
  • Provider controls: revoke access through your Google Account connections. Drive settings provide controls for app data; the hidden storage is not a normal My Drive folder. Apple provides account/storage controls, but available CloudKit deletion controls must be checked for your account. Ask for guidance before deleting recovery data. I cannot promise direct access to or deletion of private provider storage.
  • Support correspondence: I will manually review resolved cases and delete messages and attachments from the support mailbox, including its trash, and any downloaded copies under my control no later than 12 months after resolution, unless longer retention is legally necessary. I will review unresolved cases and remove information no longer needed. Any legally required extension will be limited to the relevant information and reviewed when that requirement ends. This is an operator procedure, not automated app deletion; archiving a message is not deletion. Google controls the subsequent removal of its own backup copies under its policies.
  • Beta diagnostics: Apple controls TestFlight retention. Apple states beta feedback is retained for one year and crash/usage data may be retained until bugs are resolved. I will delete or anonymise diagnostic copies I hold when no longer needed for the issue being investigated; copies retained for resolved support cases follow the 12-month limit above. I cannot erase Apple's independently retained records.

Purposes and UK GDPR lawful bases

I am the controller for the purposes and means of processing I determine for FRCS Companion, including my handling of support and beta feedback. This responsibility is distinct from whether I can access a particular record: local study records and private cloud backups are not routinely sent to me. For processing I control, the bases are:

  • Study tools and requested optional functions: legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)) in providing the organiser, local recovery, widgets, exports and the backup or service connection you choose. This includes the account identifiers and backup receipts needed to keep a selected destination separate from other accounts. Optional transfers remain off until you choose them; I do not use their contents for separate developer analytics.
  • Support correspondence: legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)) in answering your request, resolving faults and keeping enough correspondence to follow up on the case, subject to the retention limit above.
  • Beta feedback, crash information and security: legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)) in diagnosing defects and protecting the app and users. This covers my use of information Apple makes available to me, not Apple's separate collection or purposes.
  • Privacy-rights requests and legally required records: legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)) where necessary to comply with applicable data-protection duties or another specific legal requirement. This is not a blanket reason to retain all correspondence.

Legitimate interests require necessity and a balance against your rights and reasonable expectations. Permission to access a Google account, an iOS permission or the tutor’s Send toggle in earlier releases is a user control; it is not, by itself, a claim that UK GDPR consent is the lawful basis for every activity.

Apple, Google and GitHub determine their own purposes for activities such as operating your provider account, platform security and their own service diagnostics. They act as independent controllers for those activities and explain their own lawful bases and rights procedures in their notices. Their role in storing or handling particular data also depends on the applicable service terms; this policy does not treat every provider activity as processing solely on my instructions. A separately configured private service must explain its own processing before you connect.

The support mailbox is ordinary consumer Gmail, not Google Workspace. Google identifies Google LLC as its controller for UK users and states that it does not act as a processor or offer a data-processing agreement for consumer Gmail. I remain responsible for my collection, use, disclosure and retention of support correspondence; Google is responsible for its own processing under its privacy policy. See Google's consumer-service privacy guidance.

No advertising, tracking system or independently configured analytics service is enabled, and I do not sell your information or use it for advertising profiles.

International processing and transfers

I operate in England. The services below can process information outside the UK, including in the United States; UK-only storage is not promised. Their published transfer mechanisms apply within their stated scope, not as a claim that FRCS Companion has signed separate contractual safeguards.

  • Google: support messages and attachments use Gmail; optional Google authentication and Drive backup also involve Google. Google describes worldwide server locations, the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework for covered transfers to Google LLC, and standard contractual clauses where required. See Google's transfer frameworks for their scope and how to request copies of clauses. This does not claim that this support mailbox has a Google Workspace agreement or UK data residency.
  • Apple: iCloud/CloudKit and TestFlight involve Apple. Apple's privacy policy describes international processing, storage generally in the United States, and standard contractual clauses for transfers of UK personal data. See Apple's privacy policy, including its international-transfer section and contact route for copies of clauses. This does not establish a particular storage country for your CloudKit records.
  • GitHub: this site uses GitHub Pages, which logs visitors' IP addresses for security. GitHub describes processing in the United States and other locations and its transfer safeguards in the GitHub privacy statement. The two pages add no cookies, analytics scripts, forms, embedded media or third-party fonts.
  • A service you configure: its endpoint and hosting determine where selected records go. Earlier releases with optional tutoring could also send messages through that service to an AI provider. No such service is supplied with the app, so no particular country or transfer safeguard can be promised. Check that operator’s notice before sending data.

Contact the privacy address above for information about transfers for processing I control. Provider notices and their privacy contacts explain safeguards for their own processing; I do not promise access to contracts or deletion controls those providers do not make available to me.

Your right to object

You have the right to object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing based on my legitimate interests. Email frcscompanion.support@gmail.com and explain which processing concerns you. I will stop that processing unless I can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds overriding your interests, rights and freedoms, or it is needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. An objection to my processing does not itself stop Apple's or Google's independent processing; their notices explain how to contact them.

Other privacy rights

Where applicable, request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability using the privacy email above. Portability applies only where its legal conditions are met; it does not generally apply to processing based on legitimate interests. Proportionate identity verification may be needed. I cannot retrieve data held only on your device or promise erasure of provider-controlled copies. You may complain to the UK's Information Commissioner's Office. This app does not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects through automated profiling.
