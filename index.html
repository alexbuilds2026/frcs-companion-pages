Support

Email frcscompanion.support@gmail.com for support or privacy requests. Include your app version/build and a short description. Never send patient details, passwords, tokens or private documents.

FRCS Companion is operated by Deepinder Singh Dhillon, an independent individual developer in England, United Kingdom. Support is handled personally; no guaranteed response time or emergency service is offered.

Internal TestFlight installation

Install Apple's TestFlight app and accept the invitation using your authorised tester account. Access is controlled by the internal testing group. If a build is unavailable or expired, contact support; an IPA cannot simply be installed as an ordinary file.

Report a problem

  1. Find the version/build in Account & Settings.
  2. Describe the action, expected result and actual result, with iPhone model and iOS version where relevant.
  3. Send TestFlight feedback or email. Check screenshots for private information first. Share feedback opens a share sheet; it does not send automatically.

Before reinstalling

Open Account & Settings → Complete backup & restore and export a complete backup to a private location. It includes current records, profile/settings and catalogued PDFs, including archived PDFs. JSON recovery snapshots do not include PDF binary files. Portable archives are not encrypted by the app.

Do not rely on local recovery surviving app deletion. Keep the original backup until a restore has been checked. An old cloud-backup confirmation does not prove the provider is reachable now.

Cloud backup and account access

In Private backup, connect and review the destination before enabling backup or restoring a version. iCloud uses your device account and private CloudKit storage; Google Drive uses Google authorisation for hidden app storage. Neither creates an FRCS Companion account. This release has no active Sign in with Apple login.

If storage is full or a provider is unavailable, keep local data and existing backups. Check connectivity, account, permissions and storage allowance. Do not repeatedly reinstall to fix an error. Restoring stops automatic backup; review the destination before enabling it again.

Deletion and recovery limitations

Removing or archiving a record does not erase old recovery copies. Stop automatic backup and Forget Drive authorisation do not delete remote backups. There is no in-app cloud-backup deletion or purge-all function. Provider controls are separate; request guidance before deleting recovery data. See the privacy policy.

Support correspondence is manually deleted within 12 months after resolution unless longer retention is legally necessary; this includes downloaded copies under the operator's control. It is not an automated app function and does not erase provider-held backups. You can also object to processing based on legitimate interests.

Privacy policy inside the app

Open Account & Settings → Feedback & app information → Privacy policy. The link opens this site and requires internet access. If an older build does not show it, use this site's navigation and report the build number.

Study content and optional services

This is an educational organiser, not a clinical decision tool. Example content is not official examiner guidance or a prediction of passing. Verify clinical information against appropriate current sources and do not enter identifiable patient details.

AI tutoring is disabled in this beta update. Local study and saved material remain available. Optional non-AI record synchronisation requires your separately configured HTTPS service and reviewed privacy terms; no hosted service is included. Do not paste an OpenAI API key into the app. No paid subscription or purchase flow is enabled.
